It is that time of year again, where the red carpet gets rolled out and the Academy is ready to hand out gold. This year, I was able to watch 39/50 nomintations (78%) going into Oscar night. Some of the films I missed due to them being too hard to find, or the fact that I just really didn't want to sit through *Song Sung Blue*. This Oscar season has seen a lot of drama and a lot of close races. It will surely be an interesting night tonight, and I look forward to seeing how wrong I will be with some of these predictions. So without further ado, these are my predictions and my preferences for who will win and should win the Oscars for the 98th Academy Awards.

## Best Visual Effects ##
Another year, another Avatar movie. Whenever James Cameron releases one of these films you can be sure that it will win Best Visual Effects at the Oscars. Of course, the special effects in that film were amazing, but as a movie I thought it was very bad. I am done with this franchise and I do not want to watch another one again. If Avatar didn't come out this year, *F1* could have a chance at this category, but the answer has to be *Avatar: Fire and Ash*.   

__Should Win: *Avatar: Fire and Ash*__

__Will Win: *Avatar: Fire and Ash*__

## Best Costume Design ##
This years nominees in this category are particularly underwhelming. It was interesting to see *Avatar: Fire and Ash* be nominated, considering most if not all costumes in that film were CGI. *Sinners*, *Marty Supreme*, and *Hamnet* all had good costume design, but nothing too memerable. *Frankenstein* is the clear winner of these nominations, but I would have liked to see *The Ugly Stepsister* be nominated in this category.

__Should Win: *Frankenstein*__

__Will Win: *Frankenstein*__

## Best Makeup and Hairstyling ##
This category is far less competitive than last years group of nominations. I wasn't able to see *Kokuho* so I cannot comment on its makeup and hairstyling. Out of the other nominations, I really liked *The Ugly Stepsister*. If you liked *The Substance*, I would highly recommend this film. It is a unique take on the Cinderella fairytale. *The Smashing Machine* made the Rock, look slightly less like the Rock. Is that achievement worthy of a nomination? I would say no. *Frankenstein* will likely take this award as well. Jacob Elordi's monster makeup was really neat, so I would not be mad if this one, but my personal vote is *The Ugly Stepsister*.

__Should Win: *The Ugly Stepsister*__

__Will Win: *Frankenstein*__

## Best Live Action Short ##
I saw all but one nominee in this category this year. *Two People Exchanging Saliva* created an interesting world where kissing is illegal and people pay for things with slaps to the face. *A Friend of Dorothy* was a sweet story of friendship with themes of aging. *Jane Austen's Period Drama* was my favorite of the bunch. A comedy short about a Jane Austen character trying to explain periods to a man. It was very funny and had some good social commentary. Usually the simple short with a notable actor wins in this category so I think *A Friend of Dorothy* will win.

__Should Win: *Jane Austen's Period Drama*__

__Will Win: *A Friend of Dorothy*__

## Best Documentary Short ##
This is always my favorite of the shorts categories, but this year the nominees were mostly underwhelming. *Perfectly a Strangeness* was Beautifully shot, but it was just donkey's walking around in the desert. I am still shocked this got nominated at all. *Children No More* highlighted protests from within Israel over the killing of children in Gaza, but felt the documentary left some things to desire. *Armed Only with a Camera*  tells a tragic story of a documentarian, but felt a bit all over the place. *The Devil Is Busy* showed the day in the life of a security coordinator at an abortion clinic. It was interesting to see what this woman had to deal with on a daily basis. My favorite of the bunch was *All the Empty Rooms*. This film about children lost to school shootings was so tragic and heart breaking. It really made me think about the victims of these events in a different light. I really hope this one will win.  

__Should Win: *All the Empty Rooms*__

__Will Win: *All the Empty Rooms*__  

## Best Animated Short ##
This category was a bit of a mixed bag this year. Some had very creative animation style like *Butterfly*. Others were very ugly and boring like *The Three Sisters*. *The Girl Who Cried Pearls* and *Retirement Plan* where my two favorite. *Retirement Plan* does have Domhnall Gleeson narrating, which I could see bringing more attention to this short. Often, these categories are a bit of a crapshoot.

__Should Win: *Retirement Plan*__

__Will Win: *Butterfly*__

## Best International Film ##
I was able to see 4/5 nominees for this category. Sadly, I couldn't find *Sirat* anywhere. This is a competitive category this year. The two big contenders (both best picture nominees) are *Sentimental Value* and *The Secret Agent*. *Sentimental Value* was one of my favorite movies of the year. I loved the inter-relationship drama and themes the film covered. The acting and screenplay was incredible. *The Secret Agent* really underwhelmed me. I was not a fan of its pacing or story progression. I think it did some things well, but I think *It was Just an Accident* deserved the best picture nomination over *The Secret Agent*. *The Voice of Hind Rajab* was a devastating, part documentary part dramatization of an emergency phone call made by a child in Gaza. It was a truely heart breaking film.  

__Should Win: *Sentimental Value*__

__Will Win: *Sentimental Value*__

## Best Documentary Feature ##
I only watched two of the documentary nominees this year, but both were incredible. *The Perfect Neighbor* tells an enraging story, mostly made up of police body cam footage about a neighborhood dispute that escalates to violance. The *Alabama Solution* gives an inside look into the prison system of Alabama and how horrible the condisions are. This film asked some very interesting questions about the rights of incarcerated people. Both of these films are in my top 10 of the year and I would highly recommend both of them.

__Should Win: *The Alabama Solution*__

__Will Win: *The Perfect Neighbor*__

## Best Animated Feature ##
I saw all of the nominees in this category this year. I don't think many will agree with me, but I was really underwhelmed by all of these nominees. I think many of these films had interesting premises, but the execution was very bland. *Arco* had some great world building and an interesting story hook, but I felt it didn't run with its good ideas and meandered too much. *Zootopia 2*, *Elio*, and even *KPop Demon Hunters* all felt a bit paint by number to me. *KPop Demon Hunters* is the most creative of that bunch, but I still didn't love the film. The music on the otherhand was great, and feels like it does a lot of the heavy lifting. I know the film is uber popular, but it wasn't for me. Regardless I think its likely the best of the bunch this year.

__Should Win: *KPop Demon Hunters*__

__Will Win: *KPop Demon Hunters*__

## Best Sound ##
Car go vrooooom. *F1* is the clear winner in this category and I would be shocked if anything else wins. This is one of the few locks of the year this year.

__Should Win: *F1*__

__Will Win: *F1*__

## Best Original Song ##
*Sinners* original song "I Lied to You" is enjoyable and would likely win if "Golden" from *KPop Demon Hunters* didn't come out this year. The other nominees aren't memorable at all to me.

__Should Win: "Golden" *KPop Demon Hunters*__

__Will Win: "Golden" *KPop Demon Hunters*__

## Best Score ##
I am very angry that the score from *Marty Supreme* was snubbed for a nomination in this category. It is by far my favorite score this year. Since I cannot chose that one, it is between *Sinners* and *One Battle After Another*. I really like both of these scores. Both have memorable pieces and fit each film so well. I think *Sinners* will win this category, but Jonny Greenwood deserves an Oscar win for OBAA.  

__Should Win: *One Battle After Another*__

__Will Win: *Sinners*__

## Best Production Design ##
*Frankenstein* is head and shoulders above the rest of the nominees in this category this year. I think this is another easy lock.  

__Should Win: *Frankenstein*__

__Will Win: *Frankenstein*__

## Best Film Editing ##
Usually the Academy picks the worst of the nominees in this category (cough cough *Bohemian Rhapsody*), but this year I am hoping the Academy gets it right. *One Battle After Another* is the clear winner in my book, but I could see *F1* win as a dark horse, although I think that's very unlikely.

__Should Win: *One Battle After Another*__

__Will Win: *One Battle After Another*__

## Best Cinematography ##
I think the cinematography for all of these nominations is good. *Sinners*, *Train Dreams*, and *One Battle After Another* stand above *Frankenstein* and *Marty Supreme*. *Train Dreams* had some beautiful nature shots and close ups. *Sinners* had some amazing lighting throughout the entire film. For me though, *One Battle After Another* wins this category almost solely based on the car chase climax alone. That is some of the best modern day cinematography and will be used as example in film school.

__Should Win: *One Battle After Another*__

__Will Win: *One Battle After Another*__

## Best Casting ##
A new category this year! I think this is a fun new category to bring to the Oscars. My personal choice would be *Marty Supreme*. There were so many memorable characters that were made by who was cast in those characters roles. I feel like the choices made for that cast were very inspired and creative. *Sinners* I believe has won almost all of the precursor awards in this new category so it will likely walk away as the first winner in this category.

__Should Win: *Marty Supreme*__

__Will Win: *Sinners*__

## Best Original Screenplay ##
This category is filled with good screenplays. I didn't love *Blue Moon*, but I have to admit that its dialog was written very well. *Marty Supreme* is a blast from beginning to end and has some real interesting themes and characters. *Sentimental Value*, as I mentioned previously, really blew me away with its character relationships and portrayal of complex human feelings. *It was Just an Accident* was a very engaging political thriller. *Sinners* seems to be the favorite in this category. I really enjoyed the film as a whole, but I didn't feel it was anything too special. My vote is *Marty Supreme*

__Should Win: *Marty Supreme*__

__Will Win: *Sinners*__

## Best Adapted Screenplay ##
*One Battle After Another* is the clear favorite to win, and for good reason. The dialog is written so well and there are so many exciting and interesting set pieces throughout the film. *Hamnet* is a close second for me. It is much less loud and in your face compared to OBAA, but its beauty is in what isn't said more than what is said. I was not a fan of *Train Dreams* and do not think it deserved a nominee in this category. *Frankenstein* also felt a bit like "been there done that", although there were some interesting choices made in this adaption. *Bugonia* was a crazy ride and would be an interesting win for this category, but I think OBAA has it in the bag.

__Should Win: *One Battle After Another*__

__Will Win: *One Battle After Another*__

## Best Supporting Actor ##
The acting cateogries this year are all a bit up in the air as to who will win. Sean Penn, Stellan Skarsgard, and Benicio Del Toro are my favorites in this category. I would love to see Stellan win for *Sentimental Value*, but it seems Sean Penn has a lot of momentum after winning the SAG award.

__Should Win: Stellan Skarsgard, *Sentimental Value*__

__Will Win: Sean Penn, *One Battle After Another*__

## Best Supporting Actress ##
I love that Amy Madigan was nominated for *Weapons*. *Hereditary* walked so *Weapons* could run. If Madigan won, that would be so fun. My favorite of the nominees was Igna Ibsdotter Lilleaas in *Sentimental Value*. Her performance in that film was arguably the best in the whole film, and that film was full of amazing performances. I think her performance out shines *Elle Fanning* altough she is deserving of a nod here. Teyana Taylor was great in *One Battle After Another* and I would be happy if she won as well. As for Wunmi Mosaku's performance, I thought it was a bit forgettable, so I think she is at the bottom of the nominees for me.

__Should Win: Inga Ibsdotter Lilleaas, *Sentimental Value*__

__Will Win: Amy Madigan, *Weapons*__

## Best Actor ##
This is one of the closest races we have had in this category in awhile. It seemed that Timothee Chalamet was the favorite for the longest time, but Michael B. Jordan has swooped in and taken the title of front runner. I don't think its impossible for Leo to win in this category. My personal preference is Chalamet. I think its his time to win. He was incredible in *Marty Supreme*, so much better than his nominated performance last year. I am confused how Michael B. Jordan became the new frontrunner. I felt his performance was perfectly servicable, but not anything notable. DiCaprio gave a much more memerable performance, Jordan should be third in contender at best. I am very curious to see which way this one falls this year. It is a hard one to predict.

__Should Win: Timothee Chalamet, *Marty Supreme*__

__Will Win: Michael B. Jordan , *Sinners*__

## Best Actress ##
The only nominee I have not seen is Kate Hudson for *Song Sung Blue*. That movie looked very mid so I decided to skip it, especially since there is 0% she is winning this category. I think all of the other perfomrances are very strong in this category. In my opinion this is the best group of nominees out of all categories this year. I have already praised the acting in *Sentimental Value* enough, but Renate Reinsve was master class. Emma Stone gave a great performance in *Bugonia*, but it isn't close to her best work. Jessie Buckley is the favorite and the easiest lock in any acting category this year. Her performance was so heart breaking and beautiful.

__Should Win: Jessie Buckley, *Hamnet*__

__Will Win: Jessie Buckley, *Hamnet*__

## Best Director ##
This is Paul Thomas Anderson's year. As much as I love *Marty Supreme* and Josh Safdie did an amazing job, I have to give Paul Thomas Anderson the win. He is way over do and is one of the great contemporary directors.

__Should Win: Paul Thomas Anderson, *One Battle After Another*__

__Will Win: Paul Thomas Anderson, *One Battle After Another*__

## Best Picture ##
And finally, the top dog, Best Picture. Here is my ranking of this years best picture nominees:

1. *Marty Supreme*
2. *One Battle After Another*
3. *Hamnet*
4. *Sentimental Value*
5. *Sinners*
6. *Bugonia*
7. *Frankenstein*
8. *F1*
9. *Train Dreams*
10. *The Secret Agent*

*Marty Supreme* was my favorite film of the year. I am happy it got nominated for best picture, but I think it has 0% chance of actually winning. If you haven't seen it yet, go out and see it. It is a really fun time. I think *One Battle After Another* has a strong shot at winning, but *Sinners* has been gaining a lot of momentum. It is likely between those two films. OBAA has dominated precursor awards, but *Sinners* has a lot of buzz around it based on what some voters has said. I think OBAA is the likely winner, but the Academy can be surprising.  

__Should Win: *Marty Supreme*__

__Will Win: *One Battle After Another*__
